# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

请保持尊重、理性和建设性。欢迎不同技术路线和观点，但不欢迎人身攻击、骚扰、歧视、恶意引战或泄露他人隐私。

维护者可以删除不当内容、关闭争议讨论，或限制持续破坏社区环境的账号参与。

---

## English

Please stay respectful, rational, and constructive. Different technical opinions are welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, trolling, or privacy violations are not.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close hostile discussions, or limit participation from accounts that repeatedly harm the community.
